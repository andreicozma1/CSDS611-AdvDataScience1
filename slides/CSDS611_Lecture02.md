## Optimization problems

An optimization problem is denoted with

$$
\min _{x \in \mathcal{X}} f(x) \quad \text { or } \quad \max _{x \in \mathcal{X}} f(x)
$$

The objective is a function $f: \mathcal{X} \mapsto \mathbb{R}$
The search space is a set $\mathcal{X} \subset \mathbb{R}^{d}$
The queries are points $x \in \mathcal{X}$

Optimization types are trivially inter-convertible

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min _{x \in \mathcal{X}} f(x) & =\max _{x \in \mathcal{X}} g(x) & \max _{x \in \mathcal{X}} f(x) & =\min _{x \in \mathcal{X}} g(x) \\
g(x) & =-f(x) & g(x) & =-f(x)
\end{aligned}
$$

Similar conversions are achieved by any strictly decreasing function $h: \mathbb{R} \mapsto \mathbb{R}$

$$
g=h \circ f
$$

## Paradigms of optimization

Computational schemes for the solution of optimization problems (generally) follow one of two paradigms

- In numerical optimization (e.g. Newton-Raphson, Nelder-Mead, etc) the objective is relatively cheap to compute
- In stochastic optimization (e.g. Bayesian optimization, stochastic gradient descent, etc) the objective is relatively expensive to compute

Algorithms in both paradigms proceed iteratively

- start with one or more preselected query points
- repeat as needed:
    - compute the objective values at the queries
    - decide on new queries

Within the main loop

- numerical algorithms select queries directly via the objective
- stochastic algorithms select queries indirectly via surrogates/emulators of the objective

Bayesian optimization loops are characterized by the sequential development and deployment of global probabilistic surrogates

## Characteristics of BO

Bayesian optimization loops

- rely on probabilistic surrogates of the objective ...apply Gaussian processes
- develop and refine each surrogate sequentially ...apply Bayesian updating
- select queries via the surrogates ...apply decision theory

A Gaussian process is a non-parametric probabilistic model used to model random functions
...normal/Gaussian random variables are used for uncertain scalars
...multivariate normal/Gaussian random variables are used for uncertain vectors
...Gaussian processes are used for uncertain functions
Bayesian updating is a mathematical method for modifying the probabilities or an uncertain quantity of interest as new data become available
...Bayes' rule
Decision theory is the study and application of mathematical and logical frameworks to analyze how choices are made under uncertainty
...optimal actions such as new query selection and termination criteria

## The main BO loop

The main algorithmic loop in a BO scheme

- updates data summaries
- updates the surrogate
- obtains a new acquisition function
- maximizes the acquisition function
- obtains new queries
- evaluates the objective at new queries
...expand existing data structures
...refine existing Gaussian process
...byproduct of Gaussian process
...numerical optimization
...select actions

In our context, data consist of already selected queries and past objective evaluations
A surrogate is a probabilistic model built on data to approximate the objective and provide both a prediction and a measure of its uncertainty at every query in the search space

An acquisition function is a mathematical device that uses the surrogate's predictions and uncertainty to rank every query according to its utility for future evaluation

An optimization policy is the set of rules that transform data to new query selections

## Exploration vs exploitation

The exploration vs exploitation dilemma is the core challenge in selecting queries

- Exploitation: Sampling in regions of the search space where the surrogate indicates the objective yields global optima ...the aim is to locate optimizers more accurately
- Exploration: Sampling in regions of the search space where the surrogate indicates high uncertainty
...the aim is to identify new optimizers

## Acquisition functions

An acquisition function takes the predictions and uncertainty generated by the current surrogate and converts them into a numerical score for every query in the search space
![](https://cdn.mathpix.com/cropped/2b405c86-7777-4c33-ade2-1e3d3c4ffecc-8.jpg?height=135&width=589&top_left_y=199&top_left_x=854)

The score considers the selection of subsequent queries as a rational decision under uncertainty and balances two competing drives

- What the model expects ...this is exploitation against
- How uncertain the model is ...this is exploration
